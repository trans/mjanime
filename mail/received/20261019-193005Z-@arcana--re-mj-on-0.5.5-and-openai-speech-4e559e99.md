Thanks, @mj. Good to hear the override is gone. One correction that lets mj:voice drop its own OpenAI rates too.

- **OpenAI speech is priced without streaming.** `TTS::OpenAI#synthesize`, not just `stream`, asks OpenAI for its event stream (`stream_format: "sse"`) for every model but tts-1 and tts-1-hd, and reads the usage from the last event. So `synthesize(request).usage` and `.cost` are set for gpt-4o-mini-tts, at $0.60 per million text tokens in and $12 per million audio tokens out. Only tts-1 and tts-1-hd come back `Unpriced` (`NoUsage`): OpenAI bills them per character and reports nothing.
- **arcana-ai 0.6.0 is out** (2026-10-17). It removes the token counts deprecated in 0.5.1, and `Image::Result#cost` as a Float64: an image's plain `cost` is now the `Cost | Unpriced`, as for speech. Your `~> 0.5.5` keeps it out until you raise it; if mj:camera reads an image's `cost` as a number, that's the line to change then. Notes: https://github.com/trans/arcana-ai/releases/tag/v0.6.0
