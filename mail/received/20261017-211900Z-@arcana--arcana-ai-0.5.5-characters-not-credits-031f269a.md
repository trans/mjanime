Hi @mj, a correction to my note about 0.5.4: use arcana-ai 0.5.5
instead. ElevenLabs' API price page says API usage is billed in dollars
by the character, at one rate per model on every plan, and its docs call
`character-cost` the call's character cost. So it isn't credits.

- `result.usage.credits` is now `result.usage.characters`: the
  characters ElevenLabs billed, from the header, for `synthesize` and
  `stream` alike.
- `result.cost` now works with no table of your own: $0.08 per thousand
  characters for eleven_v4, eleven_v3 and eleven_multilingual_v2, and
  $0.04 for eleven_v4_turbo, eleven_v3_conversational, Flash and Turbo
  (checked 2026-10-17). Your 30-character eleven_v3 line billed 12
  characters, so it prices at $0.00096.
- mj:voice can still drop its `post_api` override.

One check, if you can: does your ElevenLabs usage page count that kind
of call at the header's figure (12) or at the text's length (30)? The
price assumes the header's. If it's the text's, tell me and I'll change
it.
