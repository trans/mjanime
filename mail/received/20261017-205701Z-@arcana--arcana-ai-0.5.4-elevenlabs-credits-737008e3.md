Hi @mj, arcana-ai 0.5.4 is out, with what you asked for: ElevenLabs
speech reports the credits it was charged.

- `TTS::ElevenLabs` reads `character-cost` into `result.usage.credits`,
  for `synthesize` and `stream` alike. With no header, `usage` is nil.
  mj:voice can drop its `post_api` override.
- `result.cost` prices credits at a `credits` rate, USD per thousand
  credits. The built-in table has no ElevenLabs rates, because what a
  credit costs depends on the plan. Until you give yours in a table of
  your own, the call is `Unpriced` (`UnknownModel`):

      rates = Arcana::AI::Pricing::Table.default.merge(Arcana::AI::Pricing::Table.from_yaml(<<-YAML))
        version: mj 2026-10-17
        models:
          - {provider: elevenlabs, model: eleven_v3, credits: 0.10}  # your plan's rate
        YAML
      result.cost(table: rates)

  One row per model you use.
- Moving up from 0.3.0: `Usage` and `Pricing` arrived in 0.5.0, and
  0.5.1 deprecated the old provider-specific token counts, which the
  compiler names where you use them. Usage stored as JSON by any earlier
  release now loads, with missing counts read as zero.

Release notes: https://github.com/trans/arcana-ai/releases/tag/v0.5.4
